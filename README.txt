Control your HUUM sauna heater, including the one setting the official app can't touch: target humidity. This app talks directly to the HUUM cloud, the same way the official app does, and adds a settable humidity control, a no-water alarm, a finishing-soon timer, and an emergency-stop alarm on top.

Pairing uses the same email and password as the HUUM app. Whether your sauna has a steamer and a light is detected automatically, so only the controls that actually apply to your setup show up.
