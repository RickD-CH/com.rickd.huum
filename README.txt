An unofficial companion app for HUUM saunas with a UKU controller, for owners who want more than the official HUUM app currently offers. The headline addition is a settable target humidity; on top of that it adds a no-water alarm, a finishing-soon timer, profiles, a scheduled start, per-session energy tracking, Flow cards and a dashboard widget. Other HUUM controllers aren't supported.

It talks to the HUUM cloud the same way the official app does, and pairing uses the same email and password. Whether your sauna has a steamer and a light is detected automatically, so only the controls that actually apply to your setup show up.
